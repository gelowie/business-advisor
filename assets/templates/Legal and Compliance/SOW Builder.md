# SOW Builder

## Summary

Drafts a Statement of Work with enough specificity to avoid scope-creep disputes. This is a starting point for review, not a substitute for an attorney.

## Inputs

- **Client or counterparty** — [name]
- **Governing MSA, if any** — [reference it, or note this is a standalone SOW]
- **Deliverables** — [what will actually be produced]
- **Timeline or milestones** — [dates or durations]
- **Acceptance criteria** — [how "done" will be judged]
- **Payment schedule** — [amounts and when they're due, tied to milestones if possible]
- **Change-order process** — [how scope changes will be handled, if already decided]

## Deliverable

1. Reference to the governing MSA, or a note that this stands alone with its own terms
2. Specific deliverables, not vague outcomes
3. Timeline and milestones
4. Acceptance criteria for each major deliverable
5. Payment schedule tied to milestones
6. Change-order process — how scope changes are proposed, approved, and priced
7. Term and termination conditions

## Requirements

- State clearly that this is a starting draft requiring attorney review before it's signed
- Deliverables must be specific enough that "done" is unambiguous — flag anything vague and ask for detail
- Always include a change-order process, even briefly — its absence is a common source of disputes
- Don't assume payment terms if none were given — mark them as a required blank
