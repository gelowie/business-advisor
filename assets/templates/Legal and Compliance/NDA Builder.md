# NDA Builder

## Summary

Drafts a starting NDA, mutual or one-way, with every key term flagged for confirmation. This is a starting point for review, not a substitute for an attorney.

## Inputs

- **Parties** — [who is involved]
- **Direction** — [mutual — both sides disclose — or one-way — only one side discloses]
- **What's being shared** — [a general description of the confidential information and its purpose]
- **Duration of the obligation** — [for example, two to five years, or indefinite for trade secrets]
- **Governing law or jurisdiction** — [state or country, if known]
- **Known carve-outs** — [information already public, already known, or independently developed]

## Deliverable

1. Parties and the purpose of disclosure
2. Definition of confidential information
3. Obligations of the receiving party
4. Standard carve-outs — already public, already known, independently developed, required by law
5. Duration of the obligation
6. Governing law and jurisdiction
7. Signature blocks

## Requirements

- State clearly, at both the top and bottom, that this is a starting draft requiring attorney review before it's signed
- Flag every placeholder or assumption so it's obvious what still needs confirming
- Don't invent a governing law or jurisdiction if none was given — mark it as a required blank
- Keep the language as plain as possible while retaining an enforceable structure
