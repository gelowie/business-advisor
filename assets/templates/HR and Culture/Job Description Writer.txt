<summary>
Writes a job description that leads with real first-90-days ownership rather than a generic list of responsibilities.
</summary>

<inputs>
Role: [title]
Team or reporting line: [where this role sits]
First 90 days: [the specific outcomes or projects this person will own]
Must-have qualifications: [genuinely non-negotiable — keep this list short]
Nice-to-have qualifications: [helpful but not required]
Compensation, location, or remote policy: [as applicable]
</inputs>

<deliverable>
1. A one-paragraph role summary — what this person will actually do and own
2. First 90 days — three to four concrete things this person will accomplish
3. Responsibilities, grouped and specific rather than a generic bullet dump
4. Must-have qualifications, honestly separated from nice-to-haves
5. Nice-to-have qualifications
6. What makes this role or team genuinely attractive, stated specifically
</deliverable>

<requirements>
- The must-have list should be truly non-negotiable — push back if it looks inflated
- No filler phrases ("fast-paced environment," "wear many hats") without specifics behind them
- Responsibilities must be concrete enough that a candidate can picture a real day
- Keep must-haves to five or fewer where possible, to avoid shrinking the applicant pool unnecessarily
</requirements>
