# Structured Interview Rubric

## Summary

Builds a structured interview scoring rubric tied to the competencies that actually predict success in the role.

## Inputs

- **Role** — [title]
- **Success after 6-12 months** — [specific outcomes that define success in this role]
- **Past hiring mistakes for this type of role** — [what went wrong before, if known]
- **Interview format** — [number of rounds, panel or one-on-one, time per interview]

## Deliverable

1. Three to five competencies that actually predict success in this role, not generic traits
2. One concrete behavioral question per competency ("tell me about a time...")
3. What a 1 (poor), 3 (adequate), and 5 (excellent) answer looks like for each question
4. Which competencies should carry the most weight for this specific role
5. A note reminding interviewers to score independently before comparing notes as a panel

## Requirements

- Competencies must trace back to the stated success outcomes, not default to generic traits ("good communicator")
- Behavioral questions must ask about specific past incidents, not hypotheticals
- Score anchors must be concrete and observable, not vague impressions
- Cap the rubric at five competencies to keep the interview practical
