# Sales Discovery Call Script

## Summary

Writes a discovery call script that surfaces real pain and gets it quantified before any pitching happens.

## Inputs

- **What's being sold** — [product or service]
- **Typical buyer** — [role or title, and what they care about]
- **Common pain points addressed** — [list them]
- **Call length** — [for example, 30 minutes]
- **Objections usually heard** — [list them, if known]

## Deliverable

1. Opening — rapport and agenda-setting in under 30 seconds
2. Current-situation questions — how the buyer handles this today
3. Pain and cost questions — get the buyer to quantify the cost of the status quo in their own words
4. Past-attempts questions — what's already been tried, and why it fell short
5. Desired-outcome questions — what "solved" looks like to them
6. Transition to next steps — only after the pain is clearly established

## Requirements

- No pitching or feature talk before pain is established and quantified
- Questions should probe specific past incidents, not hypotheticals
- Include two to three follow-up probes per section for when the first answer is vague
- End with one specific, low-friction next step, not a generic "let me know"
