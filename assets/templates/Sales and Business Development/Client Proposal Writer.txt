<summary>
Writes a client proposal that opens with the client's problem in their own words and ties pricing to value rather than a line-item list.
</summary>

<inputs>
Client: [name or company, and industry]
Stated problem or goal: [in their words, from discovery]
Proposed scope: [what will actually be delivered]
Timeline: [expected duration or milestones]
Pricing: [amount and structure — hourly, project, or retainer]
Differentiator: [what makes this the right choice versus alternatives they're considering]
</inputs>

<deliverable>
1. The client's problem and desired outcome, restated in their own language
2. Proposed approach — how the outcome will be reached, in plain terms
3. Scope and deliverables — specific, not vague
4. Timeline with milestones
5. Investment — pricing framed against the value delivered, not presented as an isolated cost
6. Why this option — the specific differentiator, backed by evidence
7. A clear next step with a date
</deliverable>

<requirements>
- Open with the client's problem, not company background
- Every deliverable must be concrete enough that "done" is unambiguous
- The pricing section must connect cost to value, not list cost alone
- Keep it scannable — short sections, no dense blocks of text
</requirements>
