<summary>
Builds a content calendar organized around recurring pillars, so output stays consistent instead of a scramble for one-off ideas.
</summary>

<inputs>
Business: [what it does]
Target audience: [who the content is for]
Channels: [platforms in use — for example YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, blog, email]
Posting cadence: [how often per channel]
Existing pillars, if any: [recurring themes already in use]
Time horizon: [for example, four weeks or one quarter]
</inputs>

<deliverable>
1. Three to five content pillars tied to audience interest and the brand's expertise
2. Specific post ideas mapped to each pillar and channel across the given time horizon, with dates
3. For each idea, the opening hook and the call to action
4. Which pieces can be repurposed across channels from a single core asset
5. Any channel that's under- or over-loaded relative to the stated cadence
</deliverable>

<requirements>
- Every idea must map back to a pillar — no orphan ideas
- Hooks must state the outcome or tension immediately, not build up to it
- Present the calendar as a table: date, channel, pillar, idea, hook, call to action
- Don't propose more unique content than the repurposing plan can realistically support
</requirements>
