# Brand Positioning Statement

## Summary

Produces a specific, defensible brand positioning statement and a supporting persona sketch — not generic marketing language.

## Inputs

- **Brand** — [name and what it does]
- **Target customer** — [who, specifically — not "everyone"]
- **Main alternative** — [what customers currently use or do instead]
- **Key benefit** — [the single most important thing this brand delivers]
- **Reason to believe** — [proof, credential, or method backing that benefit]

## Deliverable

1. A positioning statement in the form: "For [target customer] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit], unlike [main alternative], because [reason to believe]."
2. A one-paragraph primary persona covering motivation, current workaround, and what's stopping them from switching
3. Three brand voice contrasts (for example, "confident, not arrogant"), each with one example line of copy
4. A check: would a direct competitor be able to claim this same positioning with their name swapped in? If yes, sharpen it further

## Requirements

- No filler adjectives ("innovative," "best-in-class") without a specific claim behind them
- The positioning statement should fail the name-swap test — it should only make sense for this brand
- Ground the persona in real behavior and motivation, not demographics alone
- Keep the full output to one page
