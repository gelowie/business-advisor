# Pricing Strategy Builder

## Summary

Designs a pricing or tier structure grounded in the value delivered, rather than guesswork or matching competitor prices.

## Inputs

- **What's being sold** — [product or service description]
- **Business type** — [service / digital product / physical product / brick-and-mortar]
- **Current pricing, if any** — [amount and structure]
- **Customer segments** — [who buys, and how their needs or budgets differ]
- **Value delivered** — [time saved, revenue generated, or cost avoided — with numbers if known]
- **Competitor pricing** — [what comparable options cost, if known]

## Deliverable

1. Recommended pricing model — flat, tiered, usage-based, value-based, or retainer — with reasoning
2. Tier structure if applicable — 2-4 tiers, named by outcome or persona, with what's included in each
3. Price points, justified by value delivered and willingness to pay rather than cost-plus math
4. How this compares to competitor pricing, and why the difference is defensible
5. One low-risk way to validate the pricing before fully committing

## Requirements

- Anchor pricing on the value delivered to the customer, not internal cost or copying competitors
- If pricing power is genuinely uncertain, say so and suggest how to find out rather than guessing with false confidence
- Cap tiers at three unless there's a clear reason for more
- Flag if the proposed price looks low relative to the value described
