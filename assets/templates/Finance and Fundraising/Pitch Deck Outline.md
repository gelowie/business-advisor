# Pitch Deck Outline

## Summary

Drafts a fundable pitch deck outline slide by slide, with actual content on each slide rather than just headers.

## Inputs

- **Business** — [name and a one-line description]
- **Stage** — [pre-seed / seed / Series A / bootstrapped]
- **Raise amount and use of funds** — [amount and the top 2-3 uses]
- **Traction** — [revenue, users, growth rate, or other concrete proof points]
- **Target investor** — [type of investor and what they typically prioritize]

## Deliverable

Ten to twelve slides, each with a headline and 2-4 supporting bullets:
1. One-line statement of what this is and why now
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Market size — TAM/SAM/SOM with the math shown
5. Product or demo
6. Business model — how it makes money
7. Traction — the strongest available numbers
8. Go-to-market plan
9. Competition — real positioning against real alternatives
10. Team
11. Financials and the ask
12. The ask — amount, use of funds, and the runway it buys

## Requirements

- Every claim needs a number or a specific example behind it, not an adjective
- Show the market-sizing math rather than stating a single large figure
- Never claim there are no competitors — name real or adjacent alternatives
- Flag any slide where the given information isn't enough to make a strong claim
