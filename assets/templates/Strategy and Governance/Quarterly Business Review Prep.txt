<summary>
Prepares a quarterly business review that leads with the narrative, faces misses honestly, and closes with clear asks.
</summary>

<inputs>
Business or team: [name]
Quarter: [Q_ YYYY]
Audience: [who will see this — board, leadership, team]
This quarter's numbers: [revenue, growth, churn, or whatever metrics apply]
</inputs>

<deliverable>
1. Executive summary — three sentences covering results, context, and outlook
2. Metrics vs. targets — an honest read on where the quarter fell short, not just where it succeeded
3. Wins — what worked and why, specifically
4. Misses — what didn't work, the root cause, and the corrective action being taken
5. Next quarter's initiatives — tied directly to this quarter's lessons
6. Asks — what's needed from leadership to hit next quarter's targets
</deliverable>

<requirements>
- Lead with the story; let the numbers support it rather than open with them
- Don't soften misses — name the root cause directly
- Every initiative needs an owner and a success metric
- Keep the whole review presentable in about ten minutes
</requirements>
