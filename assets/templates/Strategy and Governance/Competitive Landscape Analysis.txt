<summary>
Produces a strategic competitive analysis — positioning, market gaps, and specific moves to make, not a feature checklist.
</summary>

<inputs>
Business: [name, industry, size, core offering]
Main competitors: [3-5 named competitors]
Key differentiator: [what this business does that the others don't]
Time horizon: [near-term tactical or long-term strategic]
</inputs>

<deliverable>
1. A positioning map showing where each competitor sits on the dimensions that actually matter to buyers
2. Strengths and vulnerabilities for each competitor — specific, not generic
3. Market gaps — where real demand exists but no one is serving it well
4. Threats — what could disrupt the current position within the given time horizon
5. Three specific strategic moves to make, each with reasoning
</deliverable>

<requirements>
- Base the analysis on realistic market patterns and publicly known information
- Analyze strategic positioning and business model, not just a feature-by-feature comparison
- Recommendations must be achievable by a business of this size and stage
- Call out any assumption being made so it can be checked against reality
</requirements>
