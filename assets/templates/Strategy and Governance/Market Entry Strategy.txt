<summary>
Builds a realistic market entry plan — sizing, beachhead targeting, a first-90-days plan, and clear kill criteria.
</summary>

<inputs>
Current business: [what exists today, and its real strengths/resources]
Target market: [the market being entered]
Why now: [what changed that makes this viable today]
Budget available: [range]
Timeline: [when results need to show up]
</inputs>

<deliverable>
1. Market sizing — TAM, SAM, and SOM, with the underlying math shown
2. Entry approach — build vs. partner vs. acquire, with a recommendation and reasoning
3. Beachhead — the specific first segment to target, and why it's the right starting point
4. First 90 days — a go-to-market plan with measurable milestones
5. Risks and how to mitigate each one
6. Kill criteria — the specific, time-bound signals that mean it's time to exit or pivot
</deliverable>

<requirements>
- Be realistic about starting position as a new entrant with no existing brand recognition in this market
- Milestones must be measurable ("close 10 pilot customers by day 60," not "build awareness")
- Include specific acquisition channels with an estimated cost per customer
- Kill criteria must be concrete and tied to a date, not open-ended
</requirements>
