# SWOT Analysis and Action Plan

## Summary

Runs a SWOT analysis that goes past the obvious four lists — every point gets a strategic "so what" and a concrete "now what."

## Inputs

- **Business** — [name and a one-line description of what it does]
- **Business type** — [service / digital product / physical product / brick-and-mortar]
- **Industry and current trends** — [brief context]
- **Stage** — [early / growing / established]
- **Recent developments** — [anything notable — a launch, a market shift, a team change]

## Deliverable

1. Strengths — each backed by a specific reason, not a generic claim
2. Weaknesses — an honest assessment, including at least one uncomfortable truth
3. Opportunities — ranked by feasibility and impact given actual resources on hand
4. Threats — including ones not currently on the radar
5. For every point in all four quadrants: a "so what" (the strategic implication) and a "now what" (one specific action)

## Requirements

- No generic filler points ("innovative team," "market competition") — everything must be specific to this business and industry
- Opportunities must be grounded in resources the business actually has, not just "enter a new market"
- Weaknesses must include something leadership might not want to hear
- Apply the relevant business-type lens so the emphasis matches how this type of business actually competes
