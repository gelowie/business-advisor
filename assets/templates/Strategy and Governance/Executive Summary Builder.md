# Executive Summary Builder

## Summary

Distills raw data, notes, or findings into a one-page executive summary with a clear bottom line and a recommended action.

## Inputs

- **Audience** — [who will read this — leadership, board, investors, department heads]
- **Decision at stake** — [what needs to be decided based on this information]
- **Source material** — [paste the raw data, notes, or findings to summarize]

## Deliverable

1. Bottom line — one sentence stating the answer
2. Key findings — 3-5 bullets, most important first
3. Supporting evidence — the highlights only, not the full dataset
4. Implications — what this means for the business
5. Recommended action — what to do next, with a timeline

## Requirements

- Keep it to one page, under 400 words
- Lead with the conclusion, not the methodology behind it
- Use numbers, not adjectives ("revenue grew 23%," not "revenue grew significantly")
- If the source material is ambiguous, say so rather than presenting an uncertain read as settled
- Note in one sentence what additional data would strengthen the analysis
