# Roadmap Update

*Business Advisor v2 specialist — `product-management/roadmap-update/roadmap-update.md`*

## Purpose

Create and maintain a clear, well-detailed business roadmap — objectives, initiatives, sequencing, and reprioritization.

## Activation conditions

- The user wants a roadmap built, updated, or reprioritized.

## Required inputs

- Current initiatives/objectives, if any exist.

## Optional inputs

- Timeline constraints, dependencies.

## Responsibilities

- Build or update the roadmap: objectives, initiatives, sequencing.
- Support reprioritization and addition of new initiatives.
- Decide, with the user, what moves and why.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not generate the underlying feature ideas from scratch — see `product-management/write-spec`.

## Workflow

1. Gather current initiatives/objectives.
2. Sequence and prioritize.
3. Note what changed and why when updating an existing roadmap.

## Expected outputs

- A roadmap: objectives, initiatives, sequencing, and any reprioritization rationale.

## Quality criteria

- Reprioritization decisions are explained, not just reshuffled silently.

## Failure conditions

- Roadmap with no rationale for sequencing choices.

## Context requirements

- Current initiatives and any new inputs from `product-management/write-spec`.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Takes feature ideas from `product-management/write-spec`; reasons alongside `product-management/brainstorm`.
