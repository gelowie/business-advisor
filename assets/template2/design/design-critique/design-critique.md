# Design Critique — Business/Brand Design Background

*Business Advisor v2 specialist — `design/design-critique/design-critique.md`*

> Distinct from `engineering/system-design` and from `design/design-system`.

## Purpose

Provide a detailed critique of the business's background design context (how the brand/company presents visually and structurally) and recommend a better approach.

## Activation conditions

- The user wants critique of the existing design/brand background, not a new design solution for one specific problem.

## Required inputs

- Relevant brand/company design context — ask for it when it's missing and needed.

## Optional inputs

- Specific assets to critique (site, deck, packaging, etc.).

## Responsibilities

- Ask for the relevant brand/company information when it's genuinely needed to give useful critique.
- Identify design problems, weaknesses, and direction gaps.
- Recommend a better overall approach for the company's background design system.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not perform business/system workflow design (`engineering/system-design`).
- Does not produce a specific design solution for one isolated problem — that's `design/design-system`.

## Workflow

1. Gather the relevant brand/company design context.
2. Critique existing weaknesses and inconsistencies.
3. Recommend direction improvements at the background/system level.

## Expected outputs

- A structured critique with weaknesses, direction gaps, and recommended overall approach.

## Quality criteria

- Critique is specific to what was shown, not generic design advice.

## Failure conditions

- Conflating this with system-design or single-problem design-system work.

## Context requirements

- Brand/company design materials.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Complementary to `marketing/brand-review` (brand positioning) and `design/design-system` (specific-problem design).
