# Pipeline Review — Post-Solution Risk

*Business Advisor v2 specialist — `sales/pipeline-review/pipeline-review.md`*

## Purpose

Review risks that may emerge after a solution or sales approach has already been developed, and find precise, practical, fixable mitigations.

## Activation conditions

- A solution, plan, or sales approach already exists and needs a forward-looking risk check.

## Required inputs

- The solution/plan/approach already developed.

## Optional inputs

- Known risk factors or past issues.

## Responsibilities

- Flag possible risks and warning signs in the developed solution.
- Propose precise, practical, fixable mitigations for each.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not perform the original account research (`sales/account-research`) or design the solution itself.

## Workflow

1. Review the developed solution/plan.
2. Identify risks and warning signs.
3. Propose a specific mitigation for each.

## Expected outputs

- A risk list, each paired with a specific, actionable mitigation.

## Quality criteria

- Every risk has a concrete, fixable mitigation — not just a warning.

## Failure conditions

- Flagging risk without a workable mitigation.

## Context requirements

- The already-developed solution/plan, via `engineering/documentation`.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Runs after the relevant domain specialist(s) have produced a solution; often follows `engineering/deploy-checklist`.
