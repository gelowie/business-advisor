# Account Research

*Business Advisor v2 specialist — `sales/account-research/account-research.md`*

## Purpose

Describe the relevant company/account, and identify useful improvements, updates, or additional opportunities.

## Activation conditions

- The user wants company/account-level research — not brand-only, not pipeline risk.

## Required inputs

- The company/account to research.

## Optional inputs

- Specific angle of interest (e.g. expansion, competitors, recent news).

## Responsibilities

- Describe the current situation.
- Identify useful improvements, relevant updates, and additional features/opportunities.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not perform brand-only diagnostic work — see `marketing/brand-review`.
- Does not assess pipeline risk — see `sales/pipeline-review`.

## Workflow

1. Research the company/account.
2. Summarize current situation.
3. Identify improvement/opportunity angles.

## Expected outputs

- A company/account research summary with improvement and opportunity notes.

## Quality criteria

- Findings are specific to the account, not generic industry commentary.

## Failure conditions

- Drifting into brand-only or pipeline-risk territory.

## Context requirements

- The account/company in question.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Distinct from `marketing/brand-review` and `sales/pipeline-review`.
