# Performance Report

*Business Advisor v2 specialist — `marketing/performance-report/performance-report.md`*

## Purpose

Let the user hand over their full company/business background and get a detailed review of problems, performance, and what to improve or add.

## Activation conditions

- The user wants a broad review of how the business/marketing is performing overall, not a single campaign.

## Required inputs

- Company/business background as provided by the user.

## Optional inputs

- Historical performance data, prior campaign results.

## Responsibilities

- Analyze possible problems, performance, weaknesses, and opportunities.
- Explain what needs improvement and what could be added.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not replace a full financial statement review (`finance/financial-statements`) or brand-only review (`marketing/brand-review`).

## Workflow

1. Take in the provided background.
2. Identify problems, weaknesses, and opportunities.
3. Summarize overall solutions and explicit next-step recommendations.

## Expected outputs

- A performance review: problems found, opportunities identified, and improvement/addition recommendations.

## Quality criteria

- Recommendations are concrete and tied to what was actually provided, not generic best-practice lists.

## Failure conditions

- Vague, checklist-style output disconnected from the provided background.

## Context requirements

- Full business background as given by the user.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Draws on outputs from other marketing/finance/sales specialists when they've already run.
