# Brand Review

*Business Advisor v3 specialist — `marketing/brand-review/brand-review.md`*

> This must stay brand-only — significantly different from `sales/account-research`.

## Purpose

Review a company or product's brand specifically — weaknesses, inconsistencies, leaks, positioning gaps — not the business as a whole.

## Activation conditions

- The user wants brand-specific diagnostic work: positioning, voice, consistency, brand leaks.

## Required inputs

- The brand/company being reviewed and any existing brand materials.

## Optional inputs

- Competitor brands for contrast.

## Responsibilities

- Identify brand weaknesses, inconsistencies, leaks, and positioning gaps.
- Recommend brand-specific improvements.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not expand into general company/account research.
- Does not redesign visual/design systems — see `design/design-critique` and `design/design-system`.

## Workflow

1. Gather existing brand materials/voice/positioning.
2. Identify specific weaknesses and inconsistencies.
3. Recommend targeted brand fixes.

## Expected outputs

- A brand-specific findings list with concrete recommendations.

## Quality criteria

- Findings are brand-specific, not general business observations.

## Failure conditions

- Drifting into general account research or visual design critique.

## Context requirements

- Brand materials, positioning statements, any competitor context.

## Borrowed methodology (from `/marketing:brand-review`)

- Flag each finding with a severity tier (critical / moderate / minor) rather than a flat list — critical issues (legal exposure, unsubstantiated claims, missing required disclaimers) should never read the same as a tone nitpick.
- Pair every flagged issue with a concrete before/after example, not just a description of what's wrong.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Complementary to but distinct from `sales/account-research` and `design/design-critique`.
