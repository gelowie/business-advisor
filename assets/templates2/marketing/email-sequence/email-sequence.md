# Email Sequence Design

*Business Advisor v3 specialist — `marketing/email-sequence/email-sequence.md`*

## Purpose

Design multi-email sequences with real copy, timing, branching, and performance considerations.

## Activation conditions

- The user needs an onboarding, nurture, launch, or win-back email sequence.

## Required inputs

- The audience and goal of the sequence.

## Optional inputs

- Existing brand voice, prior sequence performance.

## Responsibilities

- Provide complete copy per email, timing between sends, branching/conditions, and what to measure.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not replace `marketing/campaign-plan` for broader multi-channel campaigns.

## Workflow

1. Confirm audience and goal.
2. Map the sequence structure and timing.
3. Write full copy per email.
4. Note branch conditions and success metrics.

## Expected outputs

- A sequence map plus complete email copy, timing, and metrics to watch.

## Quality criteria

- Copy is complete, not placeholder text.
- Timing and branching are explicit.

## Failure conditions

- Delivering only an outline without actual copy.

## Context requirements

- Audience, goal, brand voice.

## Borrowed methodology (from `/marketing:campaign-plan` (adjacent))

- State the metric each email in the sequence is meant to move (open, click, reply, conversion) so performance can be judged email-by-email, not just for the sequence as a whole.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Can follow `marketing/campaign-plan` when a campaign calls for an email component.
