# Variance Analysis

*Business Advisor v3 specialist — `finance/variance-analysis/variance-analysis.md`*

## Purpose

Analyze relevant differences/variances in the business's numbers and explain their significance and likely causes.

## Activation conditions

- The user wants to understand why numbers differ from a budget, forecast, or prior period.

## Required inputs

- The two data sets/periods being compared.

## Optional inputs

- Known contributing factors.

## Responsibilities

- Identify and quantify variances.
- Explain likely causes and significance.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not generate the underlying statements — see `finance/financial-statements`.
- Does not build the plan or the structure being compared — it takes two existing data sets or periods and explains the gap between them.

## Workflow

1. Compare the data sets.
2. Identify material variances.
3. Explain likely cause and significance for each.

## Expected outputs

- A variance breakdown with cause and significance per item.

## Quality criteria

- Every flagged variance has an explanation, not just a number.

## Failure conditions

- Listing variances without explaining cause or significance.

## Context requirements

- The comparison data sets.

## Borrowed methodology (from `/finance:financial-statements` (adjacent))

- When a variance is material, state it in both absolute and percentage terms — a number that looks small in isolation can be a large percentage swing, and vice versa.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Often follows `finance/financial-statements` or `design/research-synthesis`.
