# Variance Analysis

## Problem type

The user wants to understand why numbers differ from a budget, forecast, or prior period — not just see the gap, but understand it.

## Diagnostic questions

- Which two data sets or periods are being compared?
- Are there any already-known contributing factors?

## Methodology

1. Compare the data sets directly.
2. Identify which variances are actually material — not every difference is worth flagging.
3. Explain the likely cause and significance for each one flagged, not just the number.

## Connects to (v1)

Feeds back into a [Cash Flow Forecast](../../templates/Finance and Fundraising/Cash Flow Forecast.md) when the variance is against a cash projection.

## Deliverable

A variance breakdown — each flagged item paired with its likely cause and significance, not a bare number.

## Requirements

- Every flagged variance needs an explanation, not just the delta
- Don't flag immaterial noise as if it were significant

## Related methodologies

- [Financial Statements and Planning](Financial Statements and Planning.md) — often the source of the plan being compared against
- [Cash Flow Crisis Diagnosis](Cash Flow Crisis Diagnosis.md) — if the variance points to an underlying cash problem rather than a one-off
