# Valuation and Exit Readiness

## Problem type

The user wants to know what the business is worth, set a price for selling or raising equity, or prepare it to be sold, merged or passed on — and to understand what raises or lowers that value. Read `references/financial-core.md` §11 and `references/capitals.md`.

## Diagnostic questions

- Purpose: sell, raise equity, buy-out a partner, succession, estate/tax, or curiosity?
- Three years of financials (or whatever exists), and how clean they are (accrual, reviewed or audited, owner expenses mixed in)?
- Owner's role: could the business run without the owner for 90 days?
- Customer concentration, contract terms and recurring share of revenue?
- Growth trend and the forecast the user believes?
- Jurisdiction and expected buyer type (individual, strategic, investor)?

## Methodology

1. **Normalize earnings:** remove one-off items, pay the owner a market salary, correct non-business expenses; show the adjusted profit (SDE for small owner-operated businesses; EBITDA for larger ones).
2. **Choose methods that fit the business type** and use at least two: earnings multiple, revenue multiple (recurring-revenue software), DCF (only with a credible forecast), asset-based floor, comparable transactions.
3. **Fetch current market evidence** — recent comparable sales and multiples via live search where available; otherwise label multiples as unverified illustrative ranges.
4. **Present a range with the drivers** and a sensitivity table (change in multiple, in earnings, in discount rate).
5. **Apply value adjustments explicitly:** owner dependence, customer concentration, weak records, small scale (discounts); recurring revenue, documented systems, strong retention, defensible IP (premiums). Link to the relevant capital (human, intellectual, social).
6. **Run the readiness audit:** clean financials, documented processes, key-person succession, contracts assignable, IP properly owned and assigned, legal and tax cleanliness, no hidden liabilities, data room items.
7. **Estimate net proceeds, not just price:** deal structure (asset vs equity sale), taxes, debt payoff, earn-outs, escrow — flag for a CPA/attorney by jurisdiction.
8. **Give a 6–24 month value-building plan:** the 3–5 changes that would move value most, each with expected impact and cost.

## Deliverable

Normalized earnings; valuation range by method with assumptions; sensitivity table; value adjustments; readiness scorecard; estimated net proceeds range; value-building plan.

## Requirements

- At least two methods; a range, never a single point
- Multiples and comps are sourced and dated, or labeled unverified
- Owner dependence is addressed explicitly
- Net-proceeds estimate is flagged for professional tax/legal review
- Not presented as a formal valuation or appraisal
