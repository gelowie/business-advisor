# Financial Statements and Planning

## Problem type

The user needs financial statement structure, planning, or general financial advice for the business — broader than a specific cash flow shortfall (that's Cash Flow Crisis Diagnosis) and not a variance question (that's Variance Analysis).

## Diagnostic questions

- What's the specific financial question or planning need?
- Are there existing financials to work from, or is this starting from scratch?
- What decision will this planning actually inform?

## Methodology

1. Understand the actual financial question, not just the general area it's in.
2. Provide structured analysis, advice, or planning appropriate to the specific need.
3. Note plainly where a qualified accountant should review before the user relies on it — especially anything touching tax or compliance.

## Connects to (v1)

Feeds into a [Cash Flow Forecast](../../templates/Finance and Fundraising/Cash Flow Forecast.md) when the planning need is projection-shaped, or a [Pitch Deck Outline](../../templates/Finance and Fundraising/Pitch Deck Outline.md)'s financials slide when this is fundraising-related.

## Deliverable

Financial analysis, advice, or planning output relevant to the stated need, delivered as a starting framework and clearly flagged as such anywhere it touches tax/compliance-sensitive territory.

## Requirements

- Never present output as definitive professional advice
- Flag review-before-reliance wherever the content is tax- or compliance-sensitive

## Related methodologies

- [Variance Analysis](Variance Analysis.md) — comparing actuals to whatever plan comes out of this
- [Cash Flow Crisis Diagnosis](Cash Flow Crisis Diagnosis.md) — if the real issue turns out to be a cash shortfall rather than general planning
- [SOX Testing](SOX Testing.md) — can draw on the financial structure this produces
