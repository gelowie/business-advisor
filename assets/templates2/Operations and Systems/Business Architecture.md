# Business Architecture

*This maps how a business problem is structured and solved at the level of the business itself — not software or building architecture.*

## Problem type

A problem needs a whole-picture structural map rather than one tactical fix, usually because it touches how the business is organized to operate (who decides, how money and work flow, what depends on what), not just one isolated symptom.

## Diagnostic questions

- What is the business problem that needs architecting, and what objective should the fix serve?
- What resources, people, systems or cash are actually available or constrained?
- What has already been tried, and what happened?
- Which parts of the business depend on one person, one supplier or one customer?

## Methodology

1. **State the problem and its root cause plainly.** If the cause isn't yet established, run [Multi-Problem Diagnosis](Multi-Problem Diagnosis.md) first and reuse its verdict.
2. **Map the fix** and the resources, people and cash it requires.
3. **Define how the solution operates going forward** — owners, cadence, and the numbers that show it's working.
4. **Define concretely what prevents recurrence** — a control, a trigger, a review date, not a good intention.
5. **Tie every element back to the stated objective.** Delete anything that floats unconnected.
6. **Stress-test the structure:** if the owner, the biggest customer or the biggest supplier disappeared for 30 days, what breaks?

## Connects to (v1)

The recurrence-prevention piece often becomes an [SOP Builder](../../templates/Operations and Supply Chain/SOP Builder.md); the structural map can refresh a [SWOT Analysis and Action Plan](../../templates/Strategy and Governance/SWOT Analysis and Action Plan.md) when it reveals a strategic gap.

## Deliverable

A problem → cause → fix → ongoing operation → recurrence-prevention map, every element linked to the stated objective, plus the 30-day stress-test result.

## Requirements

- Every element ties to the stated objective
- Recurrence prevention is concrete (owner + trigger + date), never generic
- Stays at business-structure level
