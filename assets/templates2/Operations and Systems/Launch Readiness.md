# Launch Readiness

## Problem type

A plan, campaign, hire, product or workflow is about to move from design into execution, and its objectives, steps, dependencies, checks and risks need confirming first so execution isn't rushed into avoidable failure.

## Diagnostic questions

- What exactly is about to be executed, and by when?
- Is there deadline pressure, and what people, budget and tools are actually available?
- What is the cost of delay versus the cost of a failed launch?

## Methodology

1. Confirm the specific plan being executed and its success measure.
2. List objectives, ordered steps and dependencies, and mark which steps cannot be undone.
3. List the checks that must pass before go (cash covers the launch plus 60–90 days of costs; owners named; legal or compliance items cleared; supplier or capacity confirmed) and the top risks with a trigger and a response for each.
4. Define a stop-loss: the specific signal that pauses or reverses the launch.
5. State the verdict plainly: **ready**, **ready with conditions** (list them), or **not ready** (list what blocks it).

## Connects to (v1)

Runs as a readiness gate before any v1 deliverable is executed — e.g. before an [SOP Builder](../../templates/Operations and Supply Chain/SOP Builder.md) rolls out or a [Campaign Plan](../Marketing and Growth/Campaign Plan.md) goes live.

## Deliverable

A pre-launch checklist (objectives, ordered steps, dependencies, checks, risks, stop-loss) with an explicit readiness verdict.

## Requirements

- Every checklist item blocks or de-risks execution — no busywork
- The verdict is explicit, never implied
- Cash exposure of the launch is stated in numbers
- Only relevant, authorized tools are recommended
