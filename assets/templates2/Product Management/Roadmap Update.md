# Roadmap Update

## Problem type

The user wants a roadmap built, updated, or reprioritized — an ongoing decision process with the user, not a one-time scoring exercise.

## Diagnostic questions

- What are the current initiatives/objectives, if any already exist?
- Are there timeline constraints or dependencies to account for?

## Methodology

1. Gather current initiatives and objectives.
2. Sequence and prioritize them with the user.
3. When updating an existing roadmap, state clearly what changed and why — never a silent reshuffle.

## Connects to (v1)

Reasons on top of the scoring produced by the [Roadmap Prioritization Worksheet](../../templates/Product Management/Roadmap Prioritization Worksheet.md), and can start from an [MVP Scope Document](../../templates/Product Management/MVP Scope Document.md) when the roadmap begins right after an MVP.

## Deliverable

A roadmap — objectives, initiatives, sequencing — with the rationale for any reprioritization made explicit.

## Requirements

- Every reprioritization decision must be explained, not just reshuffled
