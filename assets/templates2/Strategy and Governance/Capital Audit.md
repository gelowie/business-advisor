# Capital Audit

## Problem type

A financial or operational symptom (falling margin, stalled growth, cash strain, owner burnout, rising turnover) may originate outside money — in people, relationships, knowledge, equipment or the environment. The user needs the **source** found and a rebuild plan. Read `references/capitals.md`.

## Diagnostic questions

- What is the main symptom, with numbers and dates?
- Business type and size; how many people; how dependent on the owner?
- What changed before the symptom appeared (a departure, a supplier change, equipment failure, a lost client, a new competitor, a regulation)?
- Which proxies exist already (turnover, repeat rate, referral share, utilization, downtime, energy or material cost per unit)?

## Methodology

1. **Restate the symptom as a number** (e.g. gross margin down 6 points in 12 months).
2. **Score all six capitals** — financial, manufactured, intellectual, human, social and relationship, natural — as strong / adequate / weak / unknown, each backed by at least one proxy and the evidence behind it. "Unknown" is a valid score and becomes a data request.
3. **Trace conversion chains:** which weak capital plausibly explains the symptom (e.g. high turnover → training cost and errors → lower margin)? State supporting and contradicting evidence; don't force a story.
4. **Check for capital being spent to produce short-term results** — overwork, deferred maintenance, discounting that burns customer trust, cutting training — and state which capital is being depleted.
5. **Identify the binding constraint:** the single capital whose improvement unlocks the most value now.
6. **Prescribe a rebuild plan:** actions with owner, a measurable target and date, cost in money and in other capitals, and the leading indicator to watch.
7. **Translate into money cautiously:** show the financial effect of the fix as an estimated range with assumptions; never a precise value for a non-financial capital.

## Connects to (v1)

Feeds a [SWOT Analysis and Action Plan](../../templates/Strategy and Governance/SWOT Analysis and Action Plan.md) (strengths and weaknesses grounded in capitals) and, for the human and intellectual findings, a [SOP Builder](../../templates/Operations and Supply Chain/SOP Builder.md) or [Job Description Writer](../../templates/HR and Culture/Job Description Writer.md).

## Deliverable

Six-capital scorecard with evidence; the likely source of the symptom with confidence level; the binding constraint; a rebuild plan with targets and leading indicators; estimated financial effect range.

## Requirements

- Each rating is tied to a stated proxy or labeled unknown
- No precise dollar value is assigned to a non-financial capital
- Contradicting evidence is stated alongside supporting evidence
- Trade-offs between capitals are named
