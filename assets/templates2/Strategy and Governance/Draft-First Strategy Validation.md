# Draft-First Strategy Validation

*This methodology used to live at `Engineering/Testing Strategy.md`, mislabeled as software testing strategy. Its actual content — draft cheap, get feedback, refine — belongs here. The old location now points here rather than being deleted; see its own file.*

## Problem type

The request involves building or validating a strategy, campaign, or plan where a quick first pass would let the user correct direction cheaply, before real effort is spent — e.g. a campaign strategy, a rollout strategy, a go-to-market test, or a fresh direction for a SWOT/Market Entry Strategy.

## Diagnostic questions

- What's the problem or goal the strategy needs to address?
- What constraints exist — budget, timeline, team size, channels already in use?
- How costly would it be for the user to redirect *after* a full version already exists, versus now?

## Methodology

1. Draft a short, explicitly-labeled "draft/dummy strategy" from what's known so far — genuinely quick, not a disguised final version.
2. Present it and directly ask what to keep, change, or drop.
3. Fold the feedback into a final strategy that visibly reflects it.

## Connects to (v1)

Feeds whichever v1 template matches the strategy's actual domain once finalized — a [SWOT Analysis and Action Plan](../../templates/Strategy and Governance/SWOT Analysis and Action Plan.md), a [Market Entry Strategy](../../templates/Strategy and Governance/Market Entry Strategy.md), or a [Pitch Deck Outline](../../templates/Finance and Fundraising/Pitch Deck Outline.md).

## Deliverable

A labeled draft strategy, followed by a refined final strategy once feedback has been incorporated.

## Requirements

- The draft must be genuinely quick to produce and cheap for the user to reject — not a full effort in disguise
- Never present the dummy as the final answer
- The final strategy must visibly reflect whatever feedback was given
