# Deploy Checklist — Pre-Execution Readiness

## Problem type

A plan, campaign, or workflow is about to move from design into execution, and needs its objectives, steps, dependencies, checks, and risks confirmed first so execution doesn't get rushed.

## Diagnostic questions

- What plan or workflow is about to be executed?
- Is there deadline pressure, and what tools/team availability actually exist?

## Methodology

1. Confirm the specific plan that's about to be executed.
2. List objectives, ordered steps, and their dependencies.
3. Call out the checks that must pass and the risks that could derail execution.
4. Confirm which tools are actually relevant and authorized — use only those, and don't invent tooling requirements that weren't requested.
5. State plainly whether the plan is or isn't ready to execute.

## Connects to (v1)

Runs as a readiness gate before any v1 deliverable goes into execution — e.g. before an [SOP Builder](../../templates/Operations and Supply Chain/SOP Builder.md) or a finished [Campaign Plan](../Marketing and Growth/Campaign Plan.md) actually launches.

## Deliverable

A pre-execution checklist: objectives, ordered steps, dependencies, checks, risks, and an explicit readiness verdict.

## Requirements

- Nothing on the checklist is invented busywork — every item must actually block or de-risk execution
- The readiness verdict must be explicit, not implied
- Never recommend tools/plugins that aren't relevant or authorized
