# Campaign Plan

## Problem type

The user wants a marketing campaign plan for a product, launch, or broader business push — with real numbers tied to their actual business, not a generic checklist.

## Diagnostic questions

- What's being promoted, and what's the goal of the campaign?
- What's the budget, which channels are already in use, who's the target audience, and are there brand guidelines to follow?

## Methodology

1. Confirm scope with the user before committing to generating a full plan or significant research effort.
2. Draft the plan: objective, audience, message, channels, timeline, budget shape, success metrics.
3. Tie every part of the plan back to the specific product or operation it supports.

## Connects to (v1)

## Deliverable

A campaign plan covering objective, audience, channels, timeline, and success metrics — specific to this company's product/operations, not a generic template.

## Requirements

- Confirm scope with the user before spending significant effort on a full plan
- Never fabricate statistics or invent brand facts that weren't provided or found
