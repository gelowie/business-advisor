# Brand Review

## Problem type

The user wants brand-specific diagnostic work — positioning, voice, consistency, brand leaks — not a review of the business as a whole and not a visual/design overhaul.

## Diagnostic questions

- Which brand/company is being reviewed, and what existing brand materials are available?
- Are there competitor brands worth contrasting against?

## Methodology

1. Gather the existing brand materials, voice, and positioning.
2. Identify specific weaknesses, inconsistencies, and leaks — not general business observations.
3. Recommend targeted, brand-specific fixes.

## Connects to (v1)

Feeds a refresh of the [Brand Positioning Statement](../../templates/Marketing and Growth/Brand Positioning Statement.md) based on what the review actually found.

## Deliverable

A brand-specific findings list paired with concrete recommendations.

## Requirements

- Findings must be brand-specific, not general business commentary
- Don't drift into broader account research or visual/design-system critique
