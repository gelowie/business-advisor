# Performance Report

## Problem type

The user wants a broad review of how the business or its marketing is performing overall — not a single campaign, and not brand-only.

## Diagnostic questions

- What's the company/business background, as the user can describe it?
- Is there historical performance data or prior campaign results to work from?

## Methodology

1. Take in the background the user provides.
2. Identify problems, weaknesses, and opportunities from it.
3. Summarize overall recommendations and explicit next steps — concrete, not a generic best-practice list.

## Connects to (v1)

Compresses into an [Executive Summary Builder](../../templates/Strategy and Governance/Executive Summary Builder.md) for a busier audience, or feeds a recurring [Quarterly Business Review Prep](../../templates/Strategy and Governance/Quarterly Business Review Prep.md).

## Deliverable

Problems found, opportunities identified, and improvement/addition recommendations — concrete and tied to what was actually provided.

## Requirements

- Recommendations must be tied to what was actually provided, not a generic checklist
- Don't replace a full financial review or a brand-only review — pull in [Variance Analysis](../Finance and Fundraising/Variance Analysis.md) or [Brand Review](Brand Review.md) instead of trying to cover their ground here
