# Email Sequence Design

## Problem type

The user needs an onboarding, nurture, launch, or win-back email sequence — multiple emails working together, not a single email.

## Diagnostic questions

- Who's the audience, and what's the goal of the sequence?
- Is there an existing brand voice to match, or prior sequence performance to build on?

## Methodology

1. Confirm the audience and goal.
2. Map the sequence structure and timing between sends.
3. Write full copy for each email — not an outline.
4. Note branch conditions and what to measure.

## Connects to (v1)

Matches voice and tone against the [Brand Positioning Statement](../../templates/Marketing and Growth/Brand Positioning Statement.md), and can slot into a [Content Calendar Builder](../../templates/Marketing and Growth/Content Calendar Builder.md) when it's part of a broader content plan.

## Deliverable

A sequence map plus complete email copy (not placeholder text), explicit timing, and the metrics to watch.

## Requirements

- Copy must be complete, not a placeholder or outline
- Timing and branch conditions must be explicit, not implied
