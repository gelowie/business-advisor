# Stalled Growth Diagnosis

## Problem type

The user says something like "growth has flattened and I don't know why," "we're getting traffic/leads but they're not converting like they used to," or "what used to work isn't working anymore." This isn't a request to *build* a content calendar or positioning statement from scratch (that's v1's territory) — it's a request to explain why growth that used to work has stopped.

## Diagnostic questions

- Which stage actually flattened: top-of-funnel (traffic/leads), conversion, or retention/repeat business?
- Has anything changed in the channels being used, or has the same thing been run for a long time without change?
- Has the ideal customer/target audience shifted, or is the same message going to the same audience as before?
- Are competitors doing something different now that they weren't before?
- Is this a channel-specific problem (one source dried up) or across the board?

## Methodology

1. **Localize which stage actually stalled** — traffic-flat-but-converting-fine is a different problem than traffic-fine-but-conversion-dropped; don't treat "growth stalled" as one undifferentiated symptom.
2. **Rule out the common mechanisms for that specific stage**: channel saturation/algorithm change (top-of-funnel), message-market mismatch as the audience matures (conversion), or a changed competitive landscape undercutting positioning (either stage).
3. **Check whether the audience or the channel moved, not just the numbers** — a lot of "stalled growth" is actually "right message, wrong audience now" rather than "the tactic stopped working."
4. **Reason through a fix specific to the localized stage and cause** — a top-of-funnel channel problem needs a new channel or a changed approach to the old one; a conversion problem needs message/positioning work, not more traffic.
5. **State the trade-off** — a new channel costs time to learn and may underperform initially; repositioning risks alienating the existing audience that positioning was built for.

## Connects to (v1)

If the diagnosis points to a positioning/message mismatch, refresh the [Brand Positioning Statement](../../templates/Marketing and Growth/Brand Positioning Statement.md) to match who the audience actually is now, not who it was when growth was working. If the diagnosis points to channel/content fatigue rather than positioning, rebuild the [Content Calendar Builder](../../templates/Marketing and Growth/Content Calendar Builder.md) around the new channel or angle rather than more of what already plateaued.

## Deliverable

1. **Where it actually stalled** — the specific funnel stage, not "growth" broadly
2. **Why** — the mechanism, tied to the diagnostic answers
3. **The fix** — specific to that stage and cause
4. **The trade-off** — what the fix risks or costs
5. **The connected v1 output** — whichever template actually matches the diagnosis

## Requirements

- Don't recommend "post more content" or "try a new channel" without the diagnosis actually localizing where the stall is
- If traffic and conversion both look fine and only revenue is flat, check retention/repeat-purchase before assuming an acquisition problem at all
- Keep the connected v1 template's content specific to this diagnosis, not a generic version of that template
