# V2 (templates2) Index — NOT YET BUILT

This file is a structural placeholder, not a completed index. No V2
methodology files currently exist in this package.

Per the frozen V3 architecture, V2 is meant to hold moderate-depth,
single-problem diagnostic methodology across the same 8 domains as V1
(Strategy, Finance, Product, Marketing, Sales, Operations, HR, Legal) —
with HR and Legal specifically flagged as needing authorship (this was a
known gap even in prior design discussion, and remains unaddressed here
since no templates2 content exists yet at all).

This index's real job, once V2 methodology files exist, is to map each
domain to its moderate-depth methodology file(s), the same way
templates/index.md does for V1. Until then, routing.md and selection.md
will find nothing here and will fall through to templates/ (V1) as support
for any V2-tier request, per selection.md's "no-match fallback" rule.
