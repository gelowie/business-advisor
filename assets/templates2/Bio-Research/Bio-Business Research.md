# Bio-Business Research

## Problem type

The user wants a structured investigation into a possible problem within or around the business (the default case) — **or** the user's actual subject matter is genuinely biological/life-sciences, in which case this methodology shifts fully into that subject rather than forcing a business frame onto it.

## Diagnostic questions

- What aspect of the business — or, if genuinely biological, what biological topic — does the user want investigated?
- Are there prior findings from other methodologies already in hand?
- (First and most important) Is this actually a business question or a genuine biology/life-sciences question? Get this right before doing anything else.

## Methodology

1. Determine which domain actually applies — business-facing investigation, or genuine biology/life-sciences subject matter. Don't default to business framing just because this is a business-advisor skill; check what's actually being asked.
2. Apply the research approach appropriate to whichever domain applies, with the same precision either way.
3. Report findings clearly, structured for what's actually useful next.

## Connects to (v1)

Business-mode findings can feed a [SWOT Analysis and Action Plan](../../templates/Strategy and Governance/SWOT Analysis and Action Plan.md) or [Competitive Landscape Analysis](../../templates/Strategy and Governance/Competitive Landscape Analysis.md) once the investigation identifies something actionable. When the topic is genuinely biological, no v1 template applies — that's expected, not a gap.

## Deliverable

A structured findings report appropriate to whichever domain actually applies — business investigation or genuine biology topic.

## Requirements

- Correctly identify which domain applies (business vs. genuinely biological) before proceeding — this is the one step that must not be skipped or assumed
- Never force business language onto a real biology question, or vice versa
