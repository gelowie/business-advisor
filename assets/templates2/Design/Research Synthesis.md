# Research Synthesis — Financial & Statistical

## Problem type

The user needs findings pulled together from research or data into a clear, usable summary tied to a specific project or question — not raw data, and not a full financial statement build (that's [Financial Statements and Planning](../Finance and Fundraising/Financial Statements and Planning.md)).

## Diagnostic questions

- What project or topic is being investigated?
- What data or research is actually available to synthesize?
- Are there specific metrics of particular interest?

## Methodology

1. Gather the relevant financial/statistical inputs available.
2. Identify the patterns, trends, and implications that actually matter for the project's question.
3. Present a synthesized summary tied directly to that question — not a general data dump.

## Connects to (v1)

Feeds directly into a [Cash Flow Forecast](../../templates/Finance and Fundraising/Cash Flow Forecast.md) or [Pitch Deck Outline](../../templates/Finance and Fundraising/Pitch Deck Outline.md) when the synthesized numbers are meant to support one of those.

## Deliverable

A synthesized financial/statistical summary directly tied to the project's actual question.

## Requirements

- The synthesis must answer the actual project question, not present a generic data dump
- Don't perform the underlying financial statement generation here — hand that to [Financial Statements and Planning](../Finance and Fundraising/Financial Statements and Planning.md)
