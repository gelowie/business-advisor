# Design System — Specific-Problem Design

## Problem type

The user has one specific, scoped design problem — one component, one page, one asset type — not a request to review or redesign the whole business's design background (that's [Design Critique](Design Critique.md)).

## Diagnostic questions

- What's the specific design problem or topic in scope?
- Are there existing design tokens or guidelines to stay consistent with?

## Methodology

1. Confirm the specific design problem and its scope explicitly, before starting.
2. Produce a detailed solution for that one problem only.
3. Check the result stays within the confirmed scope rather than expanding into adjacent design territory.

## Connects to (v1)

No direct v1 template — this produces a scoped design asset, not a business document.

## Deliverable

A detailed, scoped design solution for the one stated problem.

## Requirements

- Stay within the confirmed scope — no drift into a full brand/business redesign
- Take direction from [Design Critique](Design Critique.md) when a critique has already identified the specific problem to solve here

## Related methodologies

- [Design Critique](Design Critique.md) — the broader review that often identifies what this should solve
