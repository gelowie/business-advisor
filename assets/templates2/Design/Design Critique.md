# Design Critique — Business/Brand Design Background

## Problem type

The user wants a critique of the business's existing design/brand background — how the brand or company presents visually and structurally overall — not a solution to one specific, scoped design problem (that's [Design System](Design System.md)), and not a business workflow design (that's [System Design](../Engineering/System Design.md)).

## Diagnostic questions

- What brand/company design context is available — site, deck, packaging, other assets?
- Are there specific assets the user wants prioritized in the critique?

## Methodology

1. Gather the relevant brand/company design context — ask for it if it's missing and actually needed to give useful critique.
2. Identify design problems, weaknesses, and direction gaps across what was shown.
3. Recommend a better overall approach at the background/system level, not a fix for one asset.

## Connects to (v1)

Findings here often point to a needed refresh of the [Brand Positioning Statement](../../templates/Marketing and Growth/Brand Positioning Statement.md) when the design gap is really a positioning gap.

## Deliverable

A structured critique: weaknesses found, direction gaps identified, and a recommended overall approach.

## Requirements

- Critique must be specific to what was actually shown, not generic design advice
- Stay at the background/direction level — don't drift into either business workflow design or one scoped design execution

## Related methodologies

- [Design System](Design System.md) — the scoped, single-problem design work this critique's findings often lead into
- [Brand Review](../Marketing and Growth/Brand Review.md) — complementary: brand positioning and voice, not visual/structural presentation
