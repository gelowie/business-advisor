# Pipeline Review — Post-Solution Risk

## Problem type

A solution, plan, or sales approach already exists for a deal and needs a forward-looking risk check before it goes further.

## Diagnostic questions

- What's the solution/plan/approach that's already been developed?
- Are there known risk factors or past issues with similar deals?

## Methodology

1. Review the developed solution or plan.
2. Identify risks and warning signs in it.
3. Propose a specific, practical, fixable mitigation for each risk — not just a warning.

## Connects to (v1)

Reviews a [Client Proposal Writer](../../templates/Sales and Business Development/Client Proposal Writer.md) draft for risk before it's sent, or flags scope/contract risk that belongs in an [SOW Builder](../../templates/Legal and Compliance/SOW Builder.md).

## Deliverable

A risk list, each item paired with a concrete, actionable mitigation.

## Requirements

- Every flagged risk needs a workable mitigation, not just a warning
- Don't perform the original account research or design the solution itself — that's [Account Research](Account Research.md)
