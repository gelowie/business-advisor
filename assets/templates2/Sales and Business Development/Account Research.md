# Account Research

## Problem type

The user wants company/account-level research — not brand-only diagnostic work, and not a risk check on a solution that already exists.

## Diagnostic questions

- Which company/account is being researched?
- Is there a specific angle of interest — expansion, competitors, recent news?

## Methodology

1. Research the company/account.
2. Summarize the current situation.
3. Identify improvement and opportunity angles specific to this account.

## Connects to (v1)

Feeds a [Sales Discovery Call Script](../../templates/Sales and Business Development/Sales Discovery Call Script.md) with real context before the call, and informs how a [Client Proposal Writer](../../templates/Sales and Business Development/Client Proposal Writer.md) frames the value.

## Deliverable

A company/account research summary with improvement and opportunity notes, specific to the account.

## Requirements

- Findings must be specific to the account, not generic industry commentary
- Don't drift into brand-only diagnostic work or pipeline risk assessment

## Related methodologies

- [Pipeline Review](Pipeline Review.md) — once a solution/approach exists for this account
