# Design System — Specific-Problem Design

*Business Advisor v3 specialist — `design/design-system/design-system.md`*

## Purpose

Provide a detailed design solution for one specific design topic or problem — not an attempt to redesign the whole business.

## Activation conditions

- The user has a specific, scoped design problem (e.g. one component, one page, one asset type).

## Required inputs

- The specific design problem or topic.

## Optional inputs

- Existing design tokens/guidelines to stay consistent with.

## Responsibilities

- Solve the one stated design problem thoroughly.

## Non-responsibilities

- Does not attempt to redesign the entire business's design background — that's `design/design-critique`'s critique role, and even it stops at critique/direction, not full execution.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the specific design problem in scope.
2. Produce a detailed solution for that problem only.

## Expected outputs

- A detailed, scoped design solution for the one stated problem.

## Quality criteria

- Solution stays within the stated scope.

## Failure conditions

- Scope creep into a full brand/business redesign.

## Context requirements

- The specific design problem and any existing design guidelines.

## Borrowed methodology (from `/design:design-system`)

- When the scoped problem touches an existing pattern, check it against a short consistency audit: naming, spacing/value tokens, and accessibility notes — the same three things a design-system audit checks for.

## Interaction with other specialists

- Takes direction from `design/design-critique` when a critique has already identified the specific problem to solve.
