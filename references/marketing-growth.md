# Marketing & Growth Hacking

## Brand Strategy

- **Positioning statements:** Use the classic form and don't skip any clause: "For [target customer] who [need/pain], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit], unlike [main alternative], because [reason to believe]." A positioning statement that could apply to any competitor with the name swapped isn't specific enough.
- **Brand voice guidelines:** Define voice with contrasts, not adjectives alone — "confident, not arrogant," "playful, not silly," "direct, not blunt." Give 2-3 real example lines of copy that hit the mark and 1-2 that miss it, so the guideline is usable, not abstract.
- **Customer persona profiling:** Anchor personas in behavior and motivation (what they're trying to accomplish, what's stopping them, where they currently look for solutions) rather than just demographics — demographics alone rarely change what marketing message lands.

## Digital Marketing

- **SEO strategy:** Prioritize by (search intent match × realistic ranking difficulty for the site's current authority), not just search volume. A page targeting a highly competitive term with no backlink profile to support it won't rank regardless of content quality.
- **PPC ad copywriting:** Lead with the specific outcome or pain point in the headline, put the differentiator in the description, and match the ad's promise exactly to the landing page's headline (mismatch kills conversion rate and quality score).
- **Email drip campaigns:** Structure around the customer's readiness stage, not a fixed number of emails — welcome/education for new leads, objection-handling and proof for warm leads, urgency/incentive for the final push. Each email needs one job; multi-purpose emails convert worse.

## Content & Social

- **Multi-channel content calendars:** Plan around pillars (3-5 recurring themes tied to the brand's expertise/audience interest) rather than one-off ideas — this keeps output consistent and makes batching easier. Repurpose one core piece of content across formats/channels rather than creating unique content per platform from scratch.
- **Video script outlines:** Hook in the first 1-3 seconds (state the outcome or the tension immediately — don't build up to it), then deliver value, then a clear single call-to-action. Cut anything that doesn't serve the hook, the value, or the CTA.
- **Community management playbooks:** Define response-time SLAs by severity (a complaint vs. a casual comment), a clear escalation path for anything reputation-sensitive, and a tone guide so different team members sound consistent.

## Analytics

- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Total sales + marketing spend over a period ÷ number of new customers acquired in that period. Segment by channel — blended CAC hides which channels are actually efficient.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** For subscription: average revenue per customer per period × gross margin % × average customer lifespan (1 ÷ churn rate). For one-time purchase: average order value × average purchase frequency × average customer lifespan × margin.
- **LTV:CAC ratio:** 3:1 or better is a common healthy benchmark; below 1:1 means the business loses money on every customer acquired, full stop, regardless of how it feels operationally.
- **Conversion rate optimization (CRO):** Change one variable at a time, run tests to a real sample-size threshold (not "it felt different after 3 days"), and prioritize testing the highest-traffic, highest-drop-off step in the funnel first.
