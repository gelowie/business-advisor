# PDF — Annotate

## Purpose

The user explicitly asks for annotations, comments, or markup on a PDF — this never happens by default just because a PDF was opened or viewed.

## What to confirm first

Exactly what should be annotated, and on which pages or sections.

## Behavior

1. Confirm exactly what annotation is wanted.
2. Apply it to the specified content only (via the environment's PDF annotation capability).
3. Confirm the result matches what was actually requested.

## What NOT to do

Never annotate a PDF just because it was viewed or opened — only on explicit request. No extra, unrequested markup.

## How this supports the business layer

A tool capability that can apply to a PDF version of any deliverable — e.g. marking up an exported NDA or a proposal draft. Doesn't touch business content or ownership.
