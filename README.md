## 🚀 What is Business Advisor Pro?

**Business Advisor Pro is not an app, a SaaS product or a standalone chatbot.** It is a **portable AI skill**: a structured knowledge base and reasoning framework written in plain Markdown, for business owners and founders.

Money is at stake in business advice, so the skill prefers a right answer over a fast confident one, without spending heavy machinery on a simple question. When it is loaded into a compatible AI assistant, the assistant identifies your business type, reads the relevant references, uses a template or a deeper methodology when one fits, and checks inputs, math and assumptions before it gives any number. Because it is plain text, you can also paste it into any AI model by hand.

**Why use it?**
A general-purpose assistant answers business questions without a fixed process. Business Advisor Pro supplies one: a fast lane for simple questions, a precision gate for anything involving money, a financial core and a six-capitals lens for finding where a problem really starts, and a jurisdiction layer so that legal and tax points are never assumed.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎯 **A precision gate:** essential inputs are confirmed, the math is checked (for example, assets = liabilities + equity), assumptions are listed, claims carry confidence labels, and volatile facts are verified or marked unverified.
- 🧮 **A financial core:** assets, liabilities, equity, revenue and expenses, the links between the three statements, ratios, unit economics by business type, working capital, capital structure, valuation and scenarios.
- 🌱 **A six-capitals lens:** financial, manufactured, intellectual, human, social and relationship, and natural capital, for symptoms that may start outside money.
- 🌐 **A jurisdiction layer:** US rules by default, plus a procedure and question set for other countries.
- ⚡ **A fast lane:** definitions, quick calculations and one-pass recommendations are answered directly, with no routing narration.
- 🏢 **4 business-type lenses** with a money-and-capital view for each: service, digital/SaaS, physical product/e-commerce and local brick-and-mortar.
- 📝 **23 fill-in templates** and 🔬 **26 deeper methodologies** for single problems, from balance-sheet health to valuation and exit readiness.
- 🧩 **A complex-problem framework** of 22 modules for several interacting problems, ending in a mandatory final consistency check.
- 📂 **File and document tools** for PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, web documents and data files.
- 🛡️ **Built-in caution:** legal, tax and compliance output is flagged as a starting draft for professional review.

## 🏢 Business Types

The skill adapts every answer to one of four business types. If the type is unclear and would change the advice, it asks one brief question. If it can be inferred, it states its assumption in passing. The full lens for each type, including a money-and-capital view, is in [`business-types.md`](references/business-types.md).

| Type | Examples | Changes the lens on... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🛠️ **Service-based** | Agencies, consultancies, freelancers, coaches, professional firms | Proposals, retainers, utilization, client concentration |
| 💻 **Digital product/SaaS** | SaaS, apps, courses, templates, newsletters | CAC/LTV, churn, retention, burn, roadmap |
| 📦 **Physical product/e-commerce** | Online stores, manufacturing, retail, dropshipping | COGS, inventory, cash conversion, fulfillment |
| 🏪 **Local brick-and-mortar** | Cafes, gyms, salons, clinics, repair shops, restaurants | Local SEO, foot traffic, labor %, occupancy, scheduling |

When domain and business-type advice conflict, the business-type lens decides emphasis and tone, not the underlying framework.

## ⚙️ How It Works

`SKILL.md` runs six steps. A request does not always need all of them.

1. **Pick the lane.** A definition, a quick calculation or a one-pass recommendation is answered directly after reading at most one reference file. Anything else continues.
2. **Identify the business type.**
3. **Identify the domains and read the references.**
4. **Use a template or methodology if one fits.**
5. **Run the precision gate** on any answer with numbers, valuations, funding, tax or legal content.
6. **Answer like a consultant:** concrete numbers, a clear next action, what would change the recommendation, and who should verify it.

`assets/routing.md` decides who owns a request, and `assets/selection.md` decides which resources the owner uses.

| Tier | Handles | Resources used |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Everyday** | Basic, document-shaped or one-pass requests | 23 templates in `assets/templates/`, with `references/` as fallback frameworks |
| **Tier 2: Diagnostic** | One problem that needs a root cause, real trade-offs or several rounds of questions | 26 methodologies in domain folders, plus the operating contract `moderate-layer.md` |
| **Tier 3: Complex** | Several interacting problems that must be decomposed and coordinated together | `assets/templates3/`, `assets/brains/` and `assets/multi-hands/` |

A deep single problem stays in Tier 2: the skill does not escalate for seriousness alone. Tier 3 may consult Tier 2 and Tier 1, and Tiers 1 and 2 may name Tier 3 only as the handoff destination for genuinely interacting problems. Tier selection is a behavioral heuristic, not a guaranteed classifier.

## 🎯 Precision Gate

[`assets/precision-gate.md`](assets/precision-gate.md) runs before any answer that contains calculations, forecasts, benchmarks, valuations, funding, tax or legal statements. The fast lane skips the full gate, but the input checks still apply when numbers are involved. The gate scales to the stakes: a one-line figure gets a one-line assumption.

| Check | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Inputs** | Confirms the essential inputs, asks once in one short batch, uses clearly labeled ranges when a figure is unknown, and never invents a figure |
| **Integrity and math** | Balance sheet balances, cash ties across statements, periods and units agree, formulas are recomputed, impossible values are caught |
| **Assumption ledger** | A short table of the assumptions that matter, their source and what happens if they are wrong |
| **Confidence labels** | **Computed**, **Benchmark**, **Judgment** or **Unverified** on key claims |
| **Volatile facts** | Rates, thresholds, deadlines, multiples and rules are verified from official sources with source and date, or marked unverified |
| **No false precision** | Rounds to the accuracy of the inputs and shows the formula |
| **Decision-ready ending** | Next action, what would change the recommendation, and who should verify it |
| **High-stakes escalation** | Adds a "what could go wrong" line and a licensed-professional review for large decisions |

## 📦 Installation

The skill is a folder with `SKILL.md` at its top level. The `name` field in `SKILL.md` is `business-advisor-pro`, so name the folder `business-advisor-pro`.

### Option A: AI tools that support skills
1. Download this repository and rename the folder to `business-advisor-pro`.
2. Place it in your tool's skills location, or use the tool's skill-upload feature if it has one.
3. Enable the skill, then ask a business question.

Each tool names and locates its skills folder differently, so check your tool's documentation.

### Option B: any AI model (manual use)
1. Paste the contents of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) into your assistant as instructions.
2. When a task needs a specific area, paste the matching file from [`references/`](references/index.md) or `assets/` as context.

## 💡 Usage & Examples

### Example prompts
- **Fast lane:** *"What is a normal profit margin for a coffee shop?"*
- **Numbers:** *"We have this much cash and this monthly burn. What is our runway?"*
- **Financial health:** *"Is my business financially healthy? Here is my balance sheet."*
- **Deep diagnostic:** *"We are profitable on paper, but there is never any cash. Why?"*
- **Funding:** *"Should I take this loan? The terms are below."*
- **Valuation:** *"What is my company worth, and how ready is it to be sold?"*
- **Complex multi-problem:** *"Sales are down, cash is tight and two key staff are leaving. Where do I start?"*
- **File analysis:** *"Read this PDF contract and summarize the payment terms."*

### Using a template directly
Every template is a Markdown file with the same five sections: **Summary, Inputs, Deliverable, Requirements, Related templates**. Fill in the Inputs and give the template to your assistant, or just describe what you need and the skill picks a matching template when one fits. This is the structure of [`SOP Builder.md`](<assets/templates/Operations and Supply Chain/SOP Builder.md>):

```markdown
# SOP Builder

## Summary
What the template produces.

## Inputs
- **Process name** — [what this covers]
- **Who performs it** — [a role, not a specific person's name]
...

## Deliverable
1. Purpose — one sentence on why this process exists
2. Trigger — when this process should be used
...

## Requirements
- Steps must be numbered and specific
...

## Related templates
- Vendor Selection Matrix
```

## 🧩 Complex-Problem Framework

Tier 3 is a set of 22 modules: an 11-module process, 10 reasoning modules and one domain-coordination module. The sequence is in [`v3-pipeline.md`](assets/templates3/v3-pipeline.md). Multi-Hands decides which domains participate in a problem; Multi-Brains reconciles different methodologies once they do. Both report their results to the pipeline without writing to shared state; the pipeline commits them. The final consistency check is the only mandatory gate at the end.

#### Process modules (`assets/templates3/`)

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Action Sequencing](assets/templates3/action-sequencing.md) | The order of actions once a decision is final. |
| [Contingency Planning](assets/templates3/contingency-planning.md) | What happens if the primary solution does not work, and which conditions trigger the fallback. |
| [Decomposition](assets/templates3/decomposition.md) | Breaks a complex or multi-issue problem into meaningful sub-problems before solving begins. |
| [Dependency Impact](assets/templates3/dependency-impact.md) | Maps relationships between components, decisions, resources or stakeholders, and what a change could affect. |
| [Escalation Conditions](assets/templates3/escalation-conditions.md) | The mid-process rule for when the evidence or process is not sufficient to continue responsibly. |
| [Final Consistency Check](assets/templates3/final-consistency-check.md) | The single mandatory gate at the end of the process, covering the whole answer. |
| [Hypothesis and Evidence](assets/templates3/hypothesis-evidence.md) | What is currently believed and which evidence supports or contradicts it. |
| [Investigation Planning](assets/templates3/investigation-planning.md) | What still needs to be found out, and how. |
| [Trade-off Comparison](assets/templates3/tradeoff-comparison.md) | Compares viable options against constraints, evidence, risk and dependencies without automatically declaring a winner. |
| [Verification Loop](assets/templates3/verification-loop.md) | How to tell whether an action worked, and what happens if it did not. |
| [Pipeline](assets/templates3/v3-pipeline.md) | The stage order and the conditions for moving between stages. It contains sequencing only; the reasoning lives in the modules. |

#### Reasoning modules (`assets/brains/`)

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|

| [Problem Selection](assets/brains/problem-selection.md) | Which sub-problem is worked first, which can run in parallel, and which are blocked. |
| [Decision Making](assets/brains/decision-making.md) | How a decision is finalized once the options have been compared. |
| [Brain Coordination (Multi-Brains)](assets/brains/brain-coordination.md) | Reconciles different methodologies when they overlap or conflict. |

| [Context Management](assets/brains/context-management.md) | Tracks which information is active right now, as distinct from the full durable state. |
| [Quality Control](assets/brains/quality-control.md) | Lightweight sanity checks that run during the process, before the final gate. |
| [Reasoning Rules](assets/brains/reasoning-rules.md) | The logical discipline every reasoning module follows, stated once. |

## ⚠️ Limitations & Disclaimer

- 🚨 **Not professional advice.** Output is a framework or starting draft, not legal, tax or investment advice. Contracts, filings and funding material should be reviewed by a qualified professional (accountant, attorney, lender) before you rely on it. The authors accept no liability for decisions made using it.
- 🌐 **Jurisdiction.** The skill applies US rules by default and uses a question-and-research procedure for other countries. It does not contain local law for other countries, so check the rules that apply to you.
- 🔎 **Volatile facts depend on search.** Rates, thresholds, deadlines and multiples are verified from official sources only when your AI tool can search. Otherwise they are labeled unverified.
- 🎲 **Routing is a heuristic.** Tier selection and the fast lane are workflow requirements written into the skill, not a technical guarantee that a model will follow them every time.
- 📉 **Uneven coverage.** HR and Culture and Legal and Compliance have templates and references but no deeper methodologies.
- 🧪 **No automated tests or benchmarks** accompany the skill.
- 📄 **File tools depend on the host.** Working with PDFs and other files requires an AI tool that can open and edit them.
- 🤖 **Quality depends on your inputs and on the AI model** that loads the skill. The precision gate asks for inputs and checks the math; it cannot verify that your figures are true.

## 🤝 Contributing

Corrections and suggestions are welcome. Please keep to these rules:

- Follow the existing template structure (Summary, Inputs, Deliverable, Requirements, Related templates).
- Keep reference content concrete. Do not state current rates, thresholds or rules from memory, and avoid invented statistics or unsourced figures.
- Do not include real client names, real financial figures or other private data in examples.
- Keep legal and tax content framed as a starting draft for professional review.
